TP Datos personales: 1,353 games of chess

Thibault Fricot - 72.74 Visualización de Información, ITBA

I started playing rapid chess seriously in August 2025. A year later I pulled every game off Chess.com through their public API: 1,353 games.

I stopped playing in January and started again at the end of May. My level came back quite fast: if the break is not too long, you do not lose much.

When did I play and what did I play?

The gap in the middle is the four months I did not play at all. I came back with a different repertoire. I was watching a streamer who plays the Scandinavian, so I already knew its ideas from the streams. The Caro-Kann was most of my Black games in the autumn and it is basically gone by summer.

At what level did I change my openings?

My rating does not move while I am away, so putting the same data against Elo instead of dates closes the gap. Three phases show up: French below 600, Caro-Kann between 600 and 1100, Scandinavian above 1200.

Each band is 100 rating points, not a fixed amount of time. I went through the low bands in a few weeks and spent months in the high ones.

Did I start thinking at a different moment?

Openings are not the only thing that moved with the rating. This one tracks where in the game my longest thinks happen.

The higher my rating, the later in the game my thinking peak comes.

Does the hour of the day change anything?

Same 1,353 games, cut by day of the week and hour of play. Color is the share I won, size is how many games the slot holds, so a large pale square is a habit that is not working.

I win 53% overall. One thing stands out, and it goes the wrong way. The slot I play the most, 16h-17h, is the slot I win the least: 202 games at 45%.

Draws count as losses here. The hour axis jumps from 1 to 7 because I have never played between 2h and 6h.

Where do my opponents come from?

I moved from France to Buenos Aires in July, so I expected the map to follow me. It does not. Argentina is 18 games in all, and half of them were played before I arrived. India, the United States and Indonesia stay on top the whole year.

The only country that reacts is France, and it moves the other way: 66 games while I lived there against 29 after I left.

134 countries in all. The country comes from the opponent's Chess.com profile, so players who leave it empty are not counted.

So what changed?

My openings changed, but they did not make me better. I left the Caro-Kann for the Scandinavian, and both give me almost the same win rate on similar numbers of games. The opening and the rating moved together, one did not cause the other. My thinking moved too: my longest thinks now come later in the game. My opponents did not follow me to Argentina, and 16h-17h is the slot I play the most and win the least.

Data and tools

All games come from the Chess.com public API. I processed them in Python and Google Sheets.

  • Openings over time and by rating: Flourish
  • Thinking time: Tableau
  • Day and hour matrix: RAWGraphs, then edited in Inkscape
  • Opponents map: Datawrapper
